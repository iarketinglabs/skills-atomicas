# Platform Playbooks

Use this reference only after channel selection. These are planning prompts, not claims about hidden ranking systems. Verify current features, policies, and reporting definitions in the relevant platform before recommending a tactic as current.

| Channel | Typical strategic strength | Plan around | Useful evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Professional relevance, expertise, business conversation | clear perspective, credible expertise, discussion quality, native presentation | target roles reached, meaningful comments, saves, qualified visits, opportunities influenced |
| YouTube | Searchable education, demonstrations, sustained attention | promise-to-delivery match, viewer usefulness, series and library design | retention patterns, returning viewers, search intent, assisted conversion |
| Instagram | Visual communication, relationship building, shareable education | original visual storytelling, saves/shares, community interaction | reach by audience, watch time, saves, shares, profile and site actions |
| TikTok | Interest-based discovery and concise native video | immediate relevance, authentic delivery, search and trend fit when relevant | early retention, completion, shares, search discovery, audience relevance |
| Substack or email publication | Owned audience, depth, recurring attention | subscription value, consistency, reader trust | opens, clicks, replies, subscriber retention, conversion to next step |
| Reddit | Community-specific conversation and research | community rules, genuine participation, usefulness before promotion | relevant thread quality, sentiment, referral quality, community feedback |

## Channel selection questions

For every candidate channel, answer:

1. Can the priority audience be reached in a context receptive to this message?
2. Does the channel fit the proof and format the organization can produce well?
3. Is there a credible next step or learning mechanism after engagement?
4. Can the team participate consistently and respectfully?
5. What result would justify keeping, expanding, reducing, or stopping it?

Choose a small core set. Put secondary channels behind a named experiment with a bounded cost and decision date.

## Platform-risk checks

- Avoid engagement bait, coordinated amplification, misleading editing, and uncredited reuse.
- Respect community rules, disclosure duties, music and media rights, accessibility needs, and local advertising rules.
- Do not move links, use a profile, or imitate a creator merely to chase a presumed distribution effect. Select the conversion path that serves the audience and can be measured.
